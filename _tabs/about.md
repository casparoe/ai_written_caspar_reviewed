This is an experimental project, where I try to see whether chatbots (such as Claude and ChatGPT) can -- with a decent amount of feedback/back-and-forth -- write blog-post-type articles that I think are somewhat reasonable and insightful.

So far this is an early-stage experiment. I don't yet know how one should relate to the articles I plan to post here, e.g., to what extent you should assume that I endorse bottom-line conclusions.
